# Thesis consistency and honesty audit

Date: 2026-10-14. Scope: the whole Quarto thesis in `reports/thesis/` (abstract, chapters 1-8, appendices A-E, `_helpers.py`, `references.bib`) read end to end against the committed results (`reports/results/*.json` and `.md`), the pre-registration and the literature full-text notes. Edited files: only `reports/thesis/*` and `docs/progress_checklist.md`; frozen files are untouched. Where a text and a committed result conflicted, the committed result won (section 3).

Evidence base used as the reference for every chapter: (1) beta_S = -0.379 pp [-0.691, -0.069], Holm p 0.036, interval excludes zero in only 4 of 11 inference variants, sign kept in 52 of 52 specifications, estimate below the minimum detectable effect (0.513 pp); (2) gamma_B equivalent within +-0.5 pp in the headline but inside the band in 0 of 11 inference variants, gamma_W not powered; (3) 1 of 10 secondary outcomes survives BH (change in CAPB); (4) no exploratory coefficient survives BH, MDE above 1 pp for all seven; (5) predictive: nothing beats persistence, the political and demographic block adds nothing; (6) CAPB revisions: mean absolute 0.64 pp, but "typical revision exceeds the SESOI" is not supported; (7) associations only, scheduled elections predetermined not exogenous.

## 3. Conflicts between text and committed results

1. Hungary listed among countries with no early election in the window; the calendar has one (1990). Fixed (3.7).
2. Carnazza et al.: political variables "explain little" versus several significant in levels in the full text. Fixed (section 6.7).
3. "Fragile to inference, not to the sample" versus 2 of 9 sample variants and one leave-one-out fit that lose zero. Fixed (5.2).
4. gamma_B "informative about smallness" versus 0 of 11 inference sensitivities inside the band. Fixed in all chapters.
5. Pre-registration/proposal "revision as large as the SESOI" versus the S4 reading (already handled in Chapter 4 and Appendix E; the frozen document is unchanged).
6. Predictive specification quotes 71% schedule agreement; the committed result gives 86.7% of 203 elections. The thesis uses the committed figure and states the correction.
7. Intro "weak-to-moderate evidence" versus conclusion "moderate and fragile" versus discussion "small negative association": three different labels for one result. Replaced by the shared statement.

## 4. Issues found and not changed

* **Pre-registration wording (frozen):** "over-rejects by about 8-10%" (section 6) and "as large as the SESOI" (section 7) cannot be edited; the thesis clarifies both in its own words.
* **Afonso et al. 0.49 pp:** the matrix records it "per the text" of the extracted PDF; whether it is a level in pp of GDP or a growth-rate effect was not re-verified (their outcome is year-on-year growth). The thesis hedges the sentence but still uses 0.49 pp as an SESOI benchmark, as pre-registered. Needs a check against the working paper.
* **Bednar's timing:** the thesis already flags the lead/lag ambiguity; I did not resolve it.
* **Literature read depth:** all studies other than Bednar, Carnazza and Afonso rest on abstracts (stated in the chapter's first paragraph). Haelg et al. (2022) uses the working-paper abstract only (note added to the bib).
* **`bibtex` notes still open:** `kayser2005`, `tepe2009`, `hainmueller2019`, `bh1995` carry "full title to verify" although the matrix shows the full titles for some of them; left as is. `mundlak1978`, `hausman1978`, `nickell1981` lack end pages (notes present).
* **Missing references:** not added (Holm; Benjamini-Yekutieli; Cameron-Gelbach-Miller 2011; equivalence testing; pre-registration; Diebold-Mariano; HLN; permutation importance; ALE). The text names Holm and Benjamini-Yekutieli procedures without citation.
* **Terminology:** variables appear as `W_S` in code spans (Appendix C) and $W^S$ in math; CAPB units are "pp of potential GDP" in results and "pp" in some running text where the unit is clear from the sentence. Not harmonised further.
* **Static captions:** the predictive table captions state "351 forecasts" and "13 yearly loss differentials" as text (verified against the JSON); the exploratory and robustness captions state B = 9,999 / 999 as text.
* **Section 5.4:** the post-election term (interval excludes zero, unadjusted) is described as uninterpreted; it is a second, larger negative association around elections and deserves a sentence in the discussion if the supervisor asks for mechanisms. Left as described (descriptive, outside every family).
* **Exploratory seed and hash provenance:** Appendix D's provenance table lists headline, secondary and robustness hashes and now the predictive hashes, but not the exploratory file (its result file records shortened hashes only).
* **Title page:** supervisor, programme, date and length limit remain TODO (checklist gap 1).
* **Not verified by reading:** the rendered figures other than the two predictive ones were not re-inspected visually; the predictive figures were viewed and match the text (block H ratios below one with indicative intervals; flat ALE curve for known pre-election exposure).
