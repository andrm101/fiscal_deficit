### 1.2 Forecast flag, latest observed year

- **No forecast flag exists.** `AMECO-METADATA` states: "The AMECO online database and text files do not include direct information on which years of a time series for a given variable and country have been forecast, which years have been sourced from official statistics and which years have been back cast". The SDMX-CSV `OBS_STATUS` column is `A` (normal) for all 1,140 UBLGBP rows; `OBS_CONF` = `F` for all. (VERIFIED-OFFICIAL, computed locally from the ECFIN SDMX-CSV.)
- **Rule for forecast years** (`AMECO-METADATA`): "The most recent available two years (Spring forecast) or three years (Autumn forecast) in the AMECO database are forecasts." Also: "in certain cases ... some data for the most recent completed year may still be forecasted" (stated for e.g. household-sector series).
- **Current vintage:** Spring 2026 (AMECO-PAGE: "Last update: 03 June 2026, with the Spring 2026 Economic Forecast"; a 21/05/2026 release preceded it; FDMS_UBLGBP stamped 2026-05-21T13:02, AMECO_CHAPTER17-01 stamped 2026-06-03T09:31). Series run to **2027**.
- **Latest observed year (decision rule):** vintage Spring 2026 minus two forecast years (2026, 2027) gives **2025 as the last non-forecast year** for EU members. Caveats: (i) CAPB is never "observed"; even the historical years are ECFIN model estimates (potential output via production function, semi-elasticities) and are revised every vintage; (ii) the 2025 value rests on 2025 outturn notified to Eurostat in spring 2026 (Eurostat gov_10a_main shows 2025 flagged `p` provisional for BE and NL); (iii) per-country exceptions to the rule are NOT VERIFIED. The Autumn 2026 release is due "mid-November" per `AMECO-METADATA` ("usually ... mid-May and mid-November") and had not appeared as of 2026-10-10 (inference from the page's 03 June 2026 last update); when it does, 2026 becomes the last non-forecast year and the Spring 2026 vintage should be archived.
- **Back-cast warning** (`AMECO-METADATA`): data before ESA 2010 availability are mechanical back-casts "with exceptions, in particular with government finance data". Users "should be aware of the limited quality of the data which have been back cast".

**Recommended employment-based (economic) old-age dependency ratio**

```
EOADR[c,t] = 100 * ( 0.5 * (P65plus[c,t] + P65plus[c,t+1]) ) / ( 1000 * E1564[c,t] )
```

- `P65plus[c,t]` = `demo_pjanbroad`, `age=Y_GE65`, `unit=NR`, `sex=T`, TIME_PERIOD = t (population on 1 January of t). The average of 1 January t and 1 January t+1 approximates mid-year population and matches the annual-average basis of LFS employment. A simpler variant using only 1 January t is acceptable if documented.
- `E1564[c,t]` = `lfsa_egan`, `unit=THS_PER`, `sex=T`, `age=Y15-64`, `citizen=TOTAL` (thousand persons; multiply by 1000).
- Interpretation: elderly persons per 100 employed persons aged 15-64. It differs from OLDDEP1 only by replacing the 15-64 population with employed 15-64.
- Sensitivities: (a) 20-64 ages (OLDDEP3 analogue) using `Y20-64`; (b) all-age employment denominator from `nama_10_pe` `EMP_DC` (national-accounts domestic concept; includes employed 65+ and cross-border workers; not age-specific); (c) denominators net of flagged `b` observations.
- Constraints: HR starts 2002 (lfsa_egan) and the LFS counts are resident-household based; breaks flagged `b` must be carried as a separate column, not dropped.

**Interest-growth differential construction (proposal).** Effective nominal interest rate `i[c,t] = UYIGE[c,t]_MIO / GD[c,t-1]_MIO` (or `D41PAY / lagged GD` from Eurostat); nominal GDP growth `g[c,t] = CP_MEUR[c,t] / CP_MEUR[c,t-1] - 1` (nominal GDP, `nama_10_gdp`). Differential `(i - g)/(1+g)`; debt-stabilising primary balance `pb* = ((i - g)/(1+g)) * d[c,t-1]` with `d` = debt/GDP. Use consistent basis (EDP interest with EDP debt).

**Crisis and shock indicators (definitions proposed, no data).**
- GFC: 1 for 2008-2009 (acute), alternative 2008-2012 window. EU sovereign-debt crisis: 1 for 2010-2013 for EL, IE, PT, ES, CY plus a euro-area-wide 2011-2012 dummy. COVID: 1 for 2020-2021 (general escape clause activated March 2020, deactivated end-2023 per Commission communications; not verified here). Energy shock: 1 for 2022-2023. Prefer year fixed effects; use these only for heterogeneity tests. All thresholds are researcher choices, not data.

## 4. Safest retrieval route (flags and negative values)

- Observed behaviour (live test 2026-10-10): Eurostat **TSV** puts value and flag in one cell, tab separated, with trailing spaces, e.g. `-5.4 b` for BE B9 2009 (`ES-SDMX/data/gov_10a_main/A.PC_GDP.S13.B9.BE?format=TSV&startPeriod=2008&endPeriod=2010` returned `-1.1 \t-5.4 b\t-4.1 `). A numeric regex that ignores the leading minus or that splits on the first non-digit will drop or corrupt negative values and flagged values. The earlier parser's loss of negatives is therefore plausibly a parsing artefact (cause in the author's parser itself not inspected).
- Recommended: **SDMX-CSV 1.0** via `format=SDMX-CSV&compressed=false`. Header observed: `DATAFLOW,LAST UPDATE,freq,unit,sector,na_item,geo,TIME_PERIOD,OBS_VALUE,OBS_FLAG,CONF_STATUS`. Negative values retained (818 negatives in the 6,390-row B9/D41PAY/TR/TE/D62PAY/D632PAY pull); flags in a separate `OBS_FLAG` column; observed flags in this pull: `p` (26 cells) and `b` (8). Key syntax: dot-separated in DSD order, `+` for several codes, empty position = all values (trailing dot for `geo`). The Eurostat query documentation (https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/web/user-guides/data-browser/api-data-access/api-detailed-guidelines/sdmx2-1/data-query) states `format=SDMX-CSV`, and that `returnData=ALL` adds series with no data (default `DATA_ONLY` omits them). Missing-value representation in SDMX-CSV is not stated there (NOT VERIFIED; my pulls contained no empty `OBS_VALUE` rows to confirm).
- JSON-stat (`ES-JSON`) is acceptable for metadata (labels) but the `status` object was absent in the small pulls; flags there are keyed by value index, so prefer SDMX-CSV for data.
- For AMECO use ECFIN SDMX-CSV (columns `OBS_STATUS`, `OBS_CONF`, `COMMENT`, `UNIT_MULT`). Note a trailing comma on FDMS CSV rows (row ends with an empty 12th field) and 5-decimal rounding in chapter flows vs full double precision in FDMS flows.

## 5. Data traps (with URLs)

1. **`UNIT=319` ambiguity.** Same code for "% of GDP (EDP)" and "% of potential GDP"; the difference is only in the `COMMENT` string. Key on the item code and COMMENT. https://webgate.ec.europa.eu/ecfin/redisstat/api/dissemination/sdmx/2.1/data/AMECO_CHAPTER17-01/A......UBLGBP?format=SDMX-CSV
2. **Output gap and cyclical-component code suffixes.** `AVGDGT`/`UBLGC`/`UBLGA`/`UBLGB`/`UUTGA` are trend-GDP variants; the potential-GDP ones are `AVGDGP`/`UBLGCP`/`UBLGAP`/`UBLGBP`/`UUTGAP`. The earlier list wrongly paired `UBLGC` and `AVGDGT` with potential GDP. https://webgate.ec.europa.eu/ecfin/redisstat/api/dissemination/sdmx/2.1/datastructure/ECFIN/DSD_AMECO/1.4?references=children
3. **`FDMS_*` vs `AMECO_CHAPTER*` code and time differences.** FDMS flows start in 2000 with 2-letter codes (EL, UK); chapter flows go back to 1975-1995 with ISO-3-like codes (GRC, GBR, ROM).
4. **No forecast flag in AMECO.** Forecast years identified only by the vintage rule (last 2 years Spring, last 3 Autumn). `OBS_STATUS`=A everywhere. https://economy-finance.ec.europa.eu/document/download/ae8a3bbf-7a9c-446c-a91e-e4e4f721c21d_en?filename=Reference_Metadata_AMECO.pdf
5. **Vintage overwrites.** The live database always shows the latest vintage; "the data in AMECO online are not dynamically updated ... between forecasts" (AMECO-METADATA). Final-vintage CAPB differs from real-time CAPB (output-gap revisions, Banco de Portugal and others document large revisions; not verified here). Archive: https://economy-finance.ec.europa.eu/economic-research-and-databases/economic-databases/ameco-database/ameco-archive_en
6. **Archive date anomaly.** "Autumn 2025" is printed with the date 3 June 2025, only 15 days after "Spring 2025" (19 May 2025) and inconsistent with the usual November Autumn release; verify the ZIP's internal date before treating it as the Autumn 2025 vintage.
7. **Methodology breaks in the CAB.** Winter 2013 (semi-elasticity, potential GDP denominator) and Autumn 2014 (new elasticities); pre-2013 vintages are not comparable. https://ec.europa.eu/economy_finance/publications/economic_paper/2014/pdf/ecp536_en.pdf
8. **Back-cast history.** AMECO values before ESA 2010 availability are mechanical (growth-rate) back-casts "with exceptions, in particular with government finance data". Do not treat early-1990s rows as outturn.
9. **Eurostat `na_item` suffix.** Use `D41PAY`, `D62PAY`, `D632PAY` in `gov_10a_main`; `D41`/`D62`/`D632` return nothing (the JSON API silently ignores unknown filter codes and returns fewer series). https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/api/dissemination/sdmx/2.1/datastructure/ESTAT/GOV_10A_MAIN/latest
10. **COFOG coverage.** SK starts 2010, FI 1990, GF1002 starts 2000/2001 for 11 states; latest year is 2024 for all but LU (2025). https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/api/dissemination/sdmx/2.1/dataflow/ESTAT/GOV_10A_EXP/latest
11. **`demo_pjan` has no `Y_GE65`.** Use `demo_pjanbroad`. `demo_pjanind` has no unit dimension (per-100 is a convention). https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/api/dissemination/statistics/1.0/data/demo_pjanbroad
12. **`lfsi_emp_a` starts 2009.** Use `lfsa_egan` (1995+ for 14 states) for a longer employment series. LFS breaks are heavily flagged `b`.
13. **HICP dataset discontinued** (`prc_hicp_aind` replaced by `prc_hicp_ainr`, ECOICOP v2). https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/api/dissemination/statistics/1.0/data/prc_hicp_ainr
14. **ParlGov is frozen.** Final release 2024-08-12 with elections only until June 2023; the maintainer retired in Oct 2024 ("The stable release is the last version of the ParlGov data"). Elections from mid-2023 (e.g. any in H2 2023) must come from ETAD (to 2023) or another source. https://parlgov.org/2024/10/01/retiring-from-parlgov/
15. **ETAD scheduling is judgement-coded**, one row per election (not country-year), no upper chambers, 264 elections with unknown scheduled date. https://yukiyanai.github.io/data_etad/ ; article PDF https://pure.uva.nl/ws/files/304890073/Election_Timing_across_Autocracy_and_Democracy.pdf
16. **CPDS variable names.** `gov_party` (not `govparty`), and codebook names carry numeric suffixes (`gov_left1`) while the dataset columns are `gov_left` etc. https://cpds-data.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/codebook_cpds.pdf
17. **IMF Fiscal Rules Dataset is de jure design** with a single coarse compliance variable (coded 0/1/2); not a compliance index series. https://www.imf.org/en/topics/fiscal-policies/fiscal-rules-dataset
18. **EC fiscal-rule index methodology file split.** Old-methodology (2016 update, dated 2017) and 2025-update files coexist; do not splice. https://economy-finance.ec.europa.eu/economic-research-and-databases/economic-databases/fiscal-governance-database_en
19. **Stale dates in sources.** DPI2023 codebook says 2025, IDB page says 2026; use the DOI https://doi.org/10.60966/8tx1xs4i. ParlGov data-info page shows "development version 4 Nov 2023" while the stable release is 2024-08-12.
20. **Redisstat old interface sunsets November 2026**; do not hard-code old `ec.europa.eu/economy_finance/ameco/...` URLs (my test of such URLs returned HTTP 404 already). https://economy-finance.ec.europa.eu/economic-research-and-databases/economic-databases/ameco-database_en
